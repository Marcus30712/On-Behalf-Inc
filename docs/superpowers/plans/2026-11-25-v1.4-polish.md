## Review Focus

1. A person covering at the exact interval their client closes/opens → label and marker follow `view` on the next refresh, never stale (Task 3 test `covering label follows the answering client`).
2. Five and seven active clients at 1280×720 → page height ≤ 720, tab bar fully visible (Task 4 browser step).
3. Expansion finishing on the last day of a month → rent prorated by days left including the finish day; month with no expansion unchanged (Task 5 test `rent prorated from completion day`).
4. Weekend client active → offer streak, hiring wait and build days advance only Mon–Fri; with no weekend client nothing changes (Task 5 tests; golden unchanged).
5. Old v1.3 save with a malformed or missing `backlog` entry → loads with an empty entry, no throw (Task 6 test).
