# v1.5 Art Refresh Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Redraw people at 16×24 with per-person variety (hair, glasses, skin, clothes) and add top-down office props, without changing the map, paths, sim or copy.

**Architecture:** All art stays as character-grid sprites in `render/sprites.js`; a person's look is chosen deterministically from their id; `render/draw.js` draws 24-tall people anchored at the tile bottom (8px overlap upward) with bottom-up ordering; props are a decorative layer listed in `render/map.js` on wall or non-walkable cells only.

**Tech Stack:** vanilla ES modules, Canvas 2D, `node --test`, jsdom, Python Playwright browser check.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-25-v1.5-art-design.md` · mockup reference `/home/claude/v1.5-art-mockup.png` (rough direction only; the user's Gemini images are reference only and never enter the repo).

## Global Constraints

- No new UI text or copy; no game names; no image files added to the repo (art is code).
- Sim untouched: `tests/fixtures/weekday-golden.json` unchanged; autoplay output unchanged.
- Map untouched: `MAP` size 20×12, `isWalkable` results, seats, desks, spots identical (pin with a test before changes).
- `tests/fixtures/phone-only-ui.json` may change only in map draw ops; DOM text identical. Regenerate only for draw ops and say so.
- Every sprite: all rows equal width, only palette letters, `.` transparent; people sprites 16×24.
- Kim Miso keeps chestnut bob, round glasses, mustard cardigan, green skirt.
- Browser check must pass; screenshots in `tools/out/` updated.
- Implementers do not commit.

## Review Focus

1. A person standing in the row under a desk or wall → head drawn over the tile above in the right order (desk in front of seated legs, head not hidden behind the wall) (Task 3 test on draw order).
2. Seated agent (`sit`/`sitBack`) at every seat orientation → body aligns with chair, no floating gap (Task 3 browser screenshot + draw-op test).
3. Two people with ids that differ by one → looks differ in at least one trait; same id → identical look across reloads (Task 2 test).
4. Props never on walkable cells or seats/doors/spots, also after expansions 1 and 2 (Task 4 test against `MAP.expansions`).
5. 💬/✉️ channel marker and "…" bubble positions follow the taller head (Task 3 test).
